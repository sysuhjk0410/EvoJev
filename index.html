EXECUTABLE EXPERIENCE, NOT WEIGHT UPDATES

Evidence constrains decisions. Experience improves the constraints.

EvoJev combines a frozen JEV classifier, evidence-bound Harness rules and bounded diagnostic search. The small model ranks candidates and actions; the large model proposes reusable rules only after a historical incident is complete.

Grounded diagnosis

Bind observations to their actual objects. Rank competing causes, acquire evidence and reject unsupported explanations.

Verified evolution

Compile the advisor’s rule into an executable predicate. Admit it only after precision, protected replay and acquisition gates pass.

Frozen verification

Stop the advisor and freeze the Harness before evaluation. Score labels only after every prediction is persisted.
